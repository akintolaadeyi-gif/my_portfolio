I'm Adeyi — a frontend developer based in Nigeria who builds interfaces that think. I specialise in the React/Next.js, turning high-fidelity designs into fast, production-ready products — from pixel to deployment.

My work sits at the intersection of design and engineering. I care deeply about how things look and how they're built. Currently at Folde Studio, I'm building the frontend of an AI-powered HR platform; shipping onboarding flows, CV management, job feeds, and employer dashboards, alongside the company's Framer website and animations.

Beyond client work, I independently designed, built, and deployed Ojú Ibojú — a full-stack Yoruba cinema streaming platform with a custom video player, AI-powered Film Creator built on the Anthropic Claude SDK, and a secured Node.js/Express backend. Solo. End to end.

I also built Canann Impact Foundation — a Nigerian NGO bridging Africa's digital divide through AI education, device access, and grassroots skills training across schools and communities.

I care about the details — performance, responsiveness, and the small interactions that make an interface feel considered. Currently exploring how AI can make web experiences smarter, not just prettier.
