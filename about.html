I'm Akin Adeyi — a frontend developer and creative technologist based in Lagos, Nigeria, working with clients and teams across the globe. I specialise in building performant, accessible, and visually compelling web interfaces.

Over five years I've shipped products used by millions: from design systems that unify engineering teams, to interactive 3D experiences that redefine how brands tell their stories online.

My process lives at the intersection of design and engineering. I care deeply about the details — micro-interactions, render performance, typographic rhythm — because the accumulation of small decisions is what separates good from memorable.

Outside of client work I contribute to open-source, mentor junior developers in Lagos, and explore emerging browser APIs.
