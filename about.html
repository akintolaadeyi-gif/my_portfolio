About

I'm Akinola — a Nigeria-based engineer focused on building web products that are useful, fast and considered.

My strongest area is frontend engineering. I enjoy taking a product from a rough idea or high-fidelity design to a responsive interface that feels right on the screen. I work mainly with React, Next.js and TypeScript.

I'm also comfortable beyond the UI. I've built Node.js/Express APIs, connected third-party services, worked with Supabase, integrated AI SDKs and handled deployment. Ojú Ibojú is a good example: I designed and shipped the product end to end, including its custom video experience and AI feature.

On client and team projects, I care about the same things: clear communication, maintainable code, sensible architecture and actually finishing what we start.
